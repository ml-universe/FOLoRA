\section*{Letter to the Editor}

Dear Editor,

We thank you and the reviewers for a second round that is unusually specific. Two of the
remaining items change what the paper claims, not just how it is written, and we have taken
both changes rather than argued around them.

\textbf{The principal table now runs O-LoRA at its training-time orthogonality objective.}
Reviewer~1 is right that reporting the initialization-only variant against methods at their
full objective was not a like-for-like comparison. The main table now carries O-LoRA at
$\lambda_1{=}1.0$, and the initialization-only variant is demoted to an ablation row in
Appendix~A. This makes our own result \emph{weaker}, not stronger: the
margin over O-LoRA falls from $+12.92$ to $+5.60$ points on CIFAR-100 and from $+13.20$ to
$+5.74$ on ImageNet-R, and the ImageNet-R average-accuracy difference after correction is
$p_{\mathrm{Holm}}{=}0.052$ --- just outside the conventional threshold. The abstract,
highlights, conclusion and results text all say so. A change to the family's membership also
moves every Holm-corrected $p$-value in the confirmatory family; all of them were recomputed
and are listed in the answer to R1-2.

\textbf{The paper no longer claims that the Fisher weighting is what produces the margin.}
Reviewer~8's item~3 is correct on the evidence we ourselves added in revision~1: a tuned
isotropic displacement penalty reaches the same accuracy, so what separates FOLoRA from the
per-task-module PEFT baselines is regularizing a \emph{shared} adapter, not the Fisher
weighting specifically. The title, abstract and highlights now say this, and the isotropic
penalty is reported as a row of the main comparison rather than only in the limitations
list. What remains attributable to Fisher weighting is narrower and is stated as such.

Two smaller points of bookkeeping. First, the revision removes the word
``pre-registered'' everywhere it was not backed by a dated record; where it is backed, the
record exists and is quoted verbatim in the answer to R8-6. Second, the TOST margin is no
longer justified by the measured reproducibility floor; it is justified by a substantive
threshold and the floor is used only as a constraint the margin must clear (R8-6).

The revision adds one new experiment (a validation-split re-selection of $\lambda$ on
CIFAR-100, R8-6, whose held-out criterion returns the main configuration), promotes one
existing experiment to the main comparison (the isotropic penalty, R1-1/R8-3), and shortens
Section~4 (R8-2). No previously reported
number was deleted or altered; where a claim became weaker, the change is itemised below.
